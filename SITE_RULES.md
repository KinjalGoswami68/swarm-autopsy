Site rules that override the design direction when they conflict:
- This is an independent project, not an Acyclic site. No Acyclic logo,
  wordmark or name used as branding. The footer says so in one line.
- Data comes first. Charts, tables and numbers are never animated into
  illegibility, never covered by ASCII layers, and meet WCAG AA contrast.
- Honour prefers-reduced-motion: all dissolve, parallax and cursor
  effects switch off, content stays fully usable.
- Works on a phone at 360px wide with no horizontal scroll. ASCII art
  scales down or is dropped on small screens.
- Holds 60fps on a mid-range laptop. ASCII effects render on canvas, pause
  when off-screen, and never block scrolling.
- Every number on the site is loaded from a run file in the repo. No
  hard-coded or illustrative figures.
